Answer the one that is waiting

Tap a session and it is the real thing, still running on the Mac. Type into it from wherever you are and the Mac carries on.

On iPhone

A keyboard with the keys a terminal needs

Escape, control, tab and the arrows, above the keyboard where a thumb can reach them. Arrange the row however you like.

Mac, iPhone and iPad

Your hosts, from whichever device you have

Save a host once and it is on your Mac, your iPhone and your iPad. The hosts travel through your own iCloud; the keys sit in the keychain, which iCloud encrypts end to end. RSA, ECDSA and Ed25519, and 1Password can hold the key and do the signing itself.

On iPad

On iPad, the Mac's own layout

Not a stretched phone app: the sidebar, the groups, the splits and the context menus, because it is the same code the Mac runs.

Groups, like your browser has

Colored and named. Collapse the ones you are not in, drag tabs between them.

Three layouts, your call

Groups down the side, tabs across the top, or both.

Splits

Put two sessions side by side. Drag a tab onto the canvas to split with it, drag the divider to resize.

Everything from the keyboard

⌘P opens a palette with every action in the app. Type a few letters, hit return.

The things a terminal owes you

Search the scrollback. Click a link to open it. Zoom. A scrollbar if you want one.

Picks up where you left off

Your tabs come back after a restart. Save a window you like and reopen it whenever.

Yours to look at

Themes that move the window and the text together. True black for OLED. Your font, your size.

A real terminal underneath

Built on Ghostty's core. Fast, true color, and everything you run behaves the way it should.

From the command line

Open any folder in a new tab without leaving the keyboard.

$ cd ~/src/checkout
$ basaltty
